Stay helps people with ADHD start what they keep avoiding.

You say the thing you are putting off. Stay gives you one small first step, waits through your pauses, and speaks first when the time is up.

  • Say it badly.

    Talk or type. Stay waits through the ums, the restarts and the half sentences, and learns on your device how long you usually pause.

  • Get one small step.

    A first move you can begin in under two minutes, a timer if you ask for one, and a spoken question when it ends: did you start?

  • It checks in, even when the app is closed.

    Two calm messages a day at most, and a ping the moment a focus block ends. Your phone writes the sentence from your own list, so the server never sees what you avoid.
